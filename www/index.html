Virtues of Istighfar

Surah Nuh (71:10-12) "Ask forgiveness of your Lord. Indeed, He is ever a Perpetual Forgiver. He will send rain upon you in showers and give you increase in wealth and children..."
Hadith (Sunan Abi Dawud 1518) "Whoever increases his prayers for forgiveness, Allah will grant him relief from every worry and a way out from every hardship..."
Hadith (Sahih al-Bukhari 6307) The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Allah, I seek forgiveness from Allah and repent to Him more than seventy times a day."

The dua chosen on the Home screen is the one counted in your current session.

App Guide & Information

Counting
Tap the big circle to count each Istighfar. Use Undo if you tap by mistake, and Reset Session to clear the current count without losing your daily total.
Anonymous Mode
Tap the mask icon in the top bar to enter Anonymous Mode. The screen simplifies to just the counter — count freely without saving progress, streaks, or history.
Setting a Goal
Tap the Goal chip above the action buttons to choose a preset (100 / 1,000 / 3,000 / 10,000) or a custom number. The ring around the counter fills as you progress, and completing your goal triggers a celebration.
Focus Mode
Tap the expand button in the top bar to enter full-screen Focus Mode. Tap anywhere on the screen to count — perfect for hands-free use during prayer.
Backup & Restore
Use Export in Settings to save your data as a .json file (shared via your phone's share sheet on Android). Use Import to restore from a backup on a new device.

Progress & Rewards

Daily Streaks
Your streak grows by 1 for every consecutive day you use the app — tracked in Settings under Streaks & achievements. Opening the app on any new day automatically archives yesterday's count to your activity history.
Ranks & Badges
Earn 15 ranks (★1–★15) as your lifetime total grows. Unlock 15 milestone badges at key counts (33, 100 … 10,000,000). Check the Insights tab to see all achievements.

Tips for Consistency

Anchor it to something you already do
Pair your Istighfar with an existing habit — right after Fajr, between sunnah and farḍ, or during your commute. It's easier to keep up a habit that's attached to another one than to remember it cold.
Start smaller than feels necessary
A low goal you actually finish every day (like 33 or 100) beats a big one you abandon halfway through. Raise your target only once the small one feels easy.
Don't let one missed day become two
A broken streak isn't a failure — it's one day. The habit that matters is coming back the next day, not the unbroken number in your streak summary.

About

Intention (Niyyah)
Do not count merely to increase numbers. This app is designed to help you track your goals and stay motivated in remembering Allah. Let your primary intention be to please Allah, not just to reach a high count. May Allah accept our efforts.
